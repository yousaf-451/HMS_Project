🏥 HMS — Hospital Management System

HMS is a frontend-only academic prototype that demonstrates how a hospital could manage patients, doctors, appointments, medical records, billing and pharmacy in one system.

Modules

  • Patient Management
  • Doctor Management
  • Appointments
  • Medical Records
  • Billing
  • Pharmacy

Prototype limits

No backend, no database, no real patient data, no real payments. All data is fictional and kept in this browser only (localStorage).
